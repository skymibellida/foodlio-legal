This policy describes how the **Foodlio** application ("the App") processes user
data. Foodlio is an app for food cost calculation and recipe/ingredient
management, designed for restaurateurs, chefs and consultants.

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller is **Kharonte** (developer of the App), reachable at
**kharonte.appdev@gmail.com**.

## 2. Core principle: your data stays on your device

Foodlio works **without an account** and **without registration**. All the
content you enter — recipes, ingredients, reports, preferences and profile data
(e.g. business name, currency) — is stored **only locally** on your device. It is
**never sent to our servers** (we operate no application server) **nor synced to
the cloud by us.**

## 3. Data processed through third-party services

To function and improve, the App uses a small number of third-party services that
may process some data in **anonymous or pseudonymous** form:

### 3.1 Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google)
To understand how the App is used and to detect malfunctions, we collect
**anonymous** data:
- **Analytics**: usage events, device type and model, OS and App version,
  language, approximate region (derived from IP address, not stored), randomly
  generated analytics identifiers.
- **Crashlytics**: crash logs, device state at the time of the error, App version
  and diagnostic data.

This data does not identify you personally and is used **solely** to improve the
App's stability and features. Processed by Google LLC as a service provider.
Privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 3.2 RevenueCat (subscription management)
In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled through **RevenueCat**, which
processes: subscription history and status, transaction identifiers, an
**anonymous user identifier** generated by the App, store country and technical
device information. RevenueCat acts as a service provider for managing
subscription entitlements. Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### 3.3 App Store (Apple) and Google Play (Google)
**Payments** are handled directly by your device's store (Apple or Google) under
their terms and privacy policies. We **do not** receive or store your card or
payment method data.

## 4. Device backup

The App's local data may be included in your **device's automatic backup**
(iCloud on iOS, Google Auto Backup on Android), managed by the operating system
according to your Apple or Google account settings. This backup is not managed by
or accessible to us.

## 5. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Provide the App's features | Local on-device content | Performance of the service |
| Improve stability and features | Anonymous analytics and crash data | Legitimate interest / consent |
| Manage purchases and subscriptions | Transaction data (RevenueCat/store) | Performance of a contract |

## 6. Data retention

- **Local data** stays on the device while you use the App and is **deleted when
  you uninstall it** (you can also clear it from the App's settings).
- **Analytics and crash data** are retained by Google according to its retention
  policies.
- **Subscription data** is retained by RevenueCat and the stores for as long as
  needed for management and accounting/tax obligations.

## 7. International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data outside the European Union (e.g. in the
United States), applying the safeguards required by applicable law (e.g. standard
contractual clauses).

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction,
objection and data portability. Since we **do not collect identifying personal
data** and operate no accounts:
- to delete your **local data**, uninstall the App or use the in-app data deletion;
- for data handled by Google/RevenueCat you can exercise your rights through their
  respective services, or by writing to **kharonte.appdev@gmail.com** and we will
  assist where possible.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their
data.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy over time. The updated version will be published at this
address with its "Last updated" date.

## 11. Contact
